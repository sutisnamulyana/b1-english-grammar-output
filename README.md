# Evaluation of AI-Generated Grammar Explanations

### Research Participation & Consent
This evaluation is part of a Master’s thesis research project investigating the quality of AI-generated grammar explanations. The goal of the study is to compare how different prompt formulations influence the quality of generated educational content. 

**Privacy & Consent:**
* Your responses will be used only for research purposes. 
* Individual ratings will be anonymized and reported only in aggregated form in the thesis. 
* Participation in this evaluation is voluntary. 
* **By continuing with this form, you confirm that you agree to participate in this research study.**

### Evaluation Criteria

Please rate each explanation using the following 1–5 scales:

#### 1. Accuracy
*Factual and rule correctness of the grammatical explanation and examples provided.*
* **5** – No factual inaccuracies in rules or examples.
* **4** – Mostly accurate explanation with very minor imprecision that does not affect understanding.
* **3** – Minor inaccuracies or imprecise formulations present.
* **2** – Noticeable inaccuracies in rules or examples that may cause some misunderstanding.
* **1** – Major rule errors or misleading examples.

#### 2. Completeness
*Coverage of the essential elements of the grammar rule and explanation appropriate for the target CEFR level.*
* **5** – All essential elements of the grammar rule are covered, including clear explanation and appropriate examples.
* **4** – Most essential elements are covered; only minor details are missing.
* **3** – Some important elements of the rule are missing or insufficiently explained.
* **2** – Several key elements of the grammar rule are missing, resulting in a partially incomplete explanation.
* **1** – The explanation is highly incomplete; major elements of the grammar rule are missing.

#### 3. Clarity / Format
*Degree to which the grammatical explanation is clearly structured, easy to understand, and logically organized for the intended learner level.*
* **5** – Explanation is very clear, well-structured, and easy to follow; formatting and examples support understanding.
* **4** – Explanation is generally clear and well organized, with only minor issues in structure or wording.
* **3** – Explanation is understandable but somewhat unclear, poorly structured, or inconsistently formatted in parts.
* **2** – Explanation is difficult to follow due to weak structure, unclear wording, or confusing organization.
* **1** – Explanation is very unclear or poorly organized, making understanding difficult.

#### 4. Relevance / Precision
*Degree to which the explanation directly addresses the requested grammar topic and remains focused on the intended instructional objective without unnecessary or unrelated information.*
* **5** – Explanation is fully focused on the requested grammar topic; all information is relevant and precisely aligned with the instructional task.
* **4** – Explanation is mostly relevant and focused, with only minor unnecessary or slightly off-topic details.
* **3** – Explanation generally addresses the topic but includes some irrelevant or loosely related information.
* **2** – Explanation only partially addresses the requested topic and includes noticeable irrelevant or unfocused content.
* **1** – Explanation is largely irrelevant to the requested grammar topic or fails to address the task appropriately.
