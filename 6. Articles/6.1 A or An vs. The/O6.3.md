## **1. A / An (Indefinite Articles)**

**Rule:** Use *a* before words starting with a consonant sound, *an* before words starting with a vowel sound.
**Usage:** When you talk about **one thing in general**, not something specific.
**Example:**

* I saw **a dog** in the park. (any dog, not a particular one)
* She wants **an apple**. (any apple, not a specific one)

---

## **2. The (Definite Article)**

**Rule:** Use *the* for **specific things** that both the speaker and listener know.
**Usage:** When you talk about **something already mentioned or unique**.
**Example:**

* I saw **the dog** you told me about. (a specific dog)
* **The sun** is very bright today. (there is only one sun)

---

✅ **Tip:**

* *A/An* = any, general.
* *The* = specific, known.
