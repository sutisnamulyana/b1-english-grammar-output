## **A / An**

**Rule:** Use *a* or *an* for **one item of something** when it is **not specific**.

* *A* is used before words starting with a **consonant sound**.
* *An* is used before words starting with a **vowel sound** (a, e, i, o, u).

**Usage:**

* We don’t know which exact thing we are talking about.
* It is the first time we mention it.

**Examples:**

* I saw **a dog** in the park. (any dog, not specific)
* She wants **an apple**. (any apple, not specific)

---

## **The**

**Rule:** Use *the* for **specific things** or things that are **already known**.

**Usage:**

* We know which thing we are talking about.
* It can be **singular, plural, or uncountable nouns**.
* Sometimes used for **unique things** or something mentioned before.

**Examples:**

* I saw **the dog** you told me about. (a specific dog)
* Please close **the window**. (a specific window, known to both speaker and listener)
* **The sun** is very bright today. (unique thing)

---

✅ **Tip:** Use *a/an* for new or unknown things, and *the* for known or specific things.
