# **A/An vs The – B1 Learner Guide**

**Rule:**

* **A/An** = **indefinite article**. Use when talking about **any one** of something, not specific.

  * **A** before consonant sounds, **An** before vowel sounds.
  * *Example:* “I saw **a** dog.” (any dog)
* **The** = **definite article**. Use when talking about **something specific** or already mentioned.

  * *Example:* “I saw **the** dog that lives next door.” (a specific dog)

**Tip:** Use **a/an** for first mention, **the** when the listener knows which one you mean.
