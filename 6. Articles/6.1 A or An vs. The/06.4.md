Here’s a simple explanation for a B1 learner about **a/an** and **the**:

---

## **1. A / An**

**Rule:** Use **a** or **an** for **one thing, not specific**.

* **A** before words starting with a **consonant sound**: a book, a car
* **An** before words starting with a **vowel sound**: an apple, an hour

**Common mistakes:**

* Using **the** when introducing something new:
  ❌ I saw **the** dog in the park. → ✅ I saw **a** dog in the park.
* Confusing **a** and **an** with spelling vs sound:
  ❌ a apple → ✅ an apple

---

## **2. The**

**Rule:** Use **the** for **specific things** or **things already mentioned**.

* Example: I saw a dog. **The dog** was very cute.
* Use with unique things: the sun, the moon, the president

**Common mistakes:**

* Using **the** too early:
  ❌ The cat is on the roof. (if first mention) → ✅ A cat is on the roof.
* Forgetting **the** for specific things:
  ❌ I liked movie we watched yesterday. → ✅ I liked **the** movie we watched yesterday.

---

**Tip for learners:**

* **A / An = any, one of many**
* **The = specific, known**
