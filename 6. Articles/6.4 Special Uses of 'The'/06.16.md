Here’s a clear explanation of the special uses of **“the”** for a B1 learner:

---

## 1. Unique things

Use **the** for something one-of-a-kind.

* Example: **the sun**, **the moon**, **the Earth**.
  **Common mistake:** Don’t say “a sun” or “a moon.”

---

## 2. Superlatives

Use **the** with words like *best*, *first*, *last*, *only*.

* Example: **the best movie**, **the first time**.
  **Common mistake:** Forgetting **the** → “best movie” is wrong here.

---

## 3. Things already mentioned

Use **the** when talking about something you already mentioned.

* Example: I saw a dog. **The dog** was very friendly.
  **Common mistake:** Saying “a dog was very friendly” again.

---

## 4. Places with names

Use **the** with rivers, oceans, seas, mountain ranges, and groups of islands.

* Example: **the Nile**, **the Pacific**, **the Alps**.
  **Common mistake:** Saying “Nile” without **the**.

---

## 5. Instruments, newspapers, and inventions

Use **the** for:

* Musical instruments: **the piano**, **the guitar**
* Newspapers: **the New York Times**
* Inventions: **the telephone**, **the internet**
