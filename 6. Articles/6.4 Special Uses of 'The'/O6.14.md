Here’s a simple explanation for a B1 learner:

---

### Special Uses of *the*

1. **Unique things:** Use *the* for one-of-a-kind things.

   * *the sun, the moon*

2. **Superlatives:** Use *the* with words like *best, first, last*.

   * *She is the best student.*

3. **Specific groups or places:** When talking about something known to the listener.

   * *I met the teacher you told me about.*

4. **Musical instruments:** Use *the* when talking about playing an instrument.

   * *He plays the piano.*

5. **Geographical uses:** Use *the* with oceans, rivers, seas, deserts, and mountain ranges.

   * *the Nile, the Alps*

---

This keeps it clear and simple for B1 learners.
