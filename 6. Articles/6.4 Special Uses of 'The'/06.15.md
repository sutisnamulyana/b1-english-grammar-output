Here’s a clear explanation of the **special uses of “the”** for a B1 learner:

---

### 1. Unique things

Use **“the”** when something is **one of a kind**.
**Example:** The sun rises in the east.

### 2. Superlatives

Use **“the”** with **-est words** or words like *best, worst, first, last*.
**Example:** She is the smartest student in class.

### 3. Things already mentioned

Use **“the”** when the listener **knows which thing you mean**.
**Example:** I saw a dog. The dog was very friendly.

### 4. Things with a defining feature

Use **“the”** when there is **only one possible choice in context**.
**Example:** Please close the door. (There is only one door in this room.)

### 5. Geographical names (special cases)

* **Use “the”** with rivers, oceans, seas, mountain ranges, and deserts.
  **Example:** The Nile, the Pacific Ocean, the Sahara
* **Don’t use “the”** with most countries, cities, or mountains.
  **Example:** France, Mount Everest
