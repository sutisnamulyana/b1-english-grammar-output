Here’s a clear explanation for a B1 learner:

---

## **No Article (Ø)**

### **Rule**

We do not use **a, an,** or **the** with certain nouns in specific situations.

---

### **1. Plural and Uncountable Nouns in General**

* **Usage:** When talking about things in general, not something specific.
* **Examples:**

  * I like **cats**. (all cats, not specific cats)
  * She drinks **water** every day. (in general, not a specific water)

---

### **2. Names of Countries, Cities, and People**

* **Usage:** Do not use an article with proper nouns.
* **Examples:**

  * **Sweden** is cold in winter.
  * **John** is my friend.

*Exceptions:* Some countries have “the” (e.g., **the Netherlands**, **the USA**).

---

### **3. Names of Languages and School Subjects**

* **Usage:** No article before languages or school subjects.
* **Examples:**

  * I study **English** and **math**.
  * She speaks **French** very well.

---

### **4. Meals, Places, and Transport (General)**

* **Usage:** When talking about routine or general activities.
* **Examples:**

  * We have **breakfast** at 7 a.m.
  * He goes to **school** every day.
  * I go to **work** by bus.

---

### **5. Abstract Ideas**

* **Usage:** Do not use an article with feelings, ideas, or concepts in general.
* **Examples:**

  * **Happiness** is important in life.
  * **Freedom** cannot be bought.
