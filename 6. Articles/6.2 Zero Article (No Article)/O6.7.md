Here’s a clear explanation for a B1 learner about **when to use no article**:

---

## **No Article (Ø) Usage**

### **1. With Plural and Uncountable Nouns in General**

* When talking about things in general, **do not use “a” or “the.”**
* **Examples:**

  * Dogs are friendly. (all dogs, not specific)
  * Water is important for life. (all water)

### **2. With Names of Places**

* Usually, we **do not use articles** with:

  * Cities: Paris, Tokyo
  * Countries: Sweden, Indonesia (except “the Netherlands,” “the USA”)
  * Continents: Asia, Europe

### **3. With Meals, Languages, and School Subjects**

* No article is needed when talking about:

  * Breakfast, lunch, dinner
  * English, Swedish, Math
  * **Example:** I like breakfast. She studies English.

### **4. With Professions or Titles**

* No article is used when stating someone’s job:

  * He is teacher. ❌ → He is **a teacher.** ✅
    *(Note: We usually need “a” here. Only no article is used with plural forms: Teachers work hard.)*

### **5. With Transport and Places in General**

* Talking generally about transport or institutions:

  * By bus, by train
  * Go to school, go to hospital (UK English)

---

✅ **Tip:** If you mean a **specific thing**, use “the.” Otherwise, no article is needed.
