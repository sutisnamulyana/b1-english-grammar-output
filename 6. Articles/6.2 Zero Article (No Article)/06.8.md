Here’s a clear explanation for a B1 learner about **using no article**:

---

## **Rule: No Article (Ø)**

We don’t use **a, an, or the** before:

### 1. **Plural and uncountable nouns when speaking generally**

* Use no article when you talk about **things in general**, not a specific thing.
* **Example:**

  * ❌ I like the books.
  * ✅ I like books.
  * ❌ I drink the water.
  * ✅ I drink water.

### 2. **Names of places, languages, and subjects**

* Countries, cities, languages, school subjects usually **don’t need an article**.
* **Example:**

  * ✅ I live in Sweden.
  * ✅ She speaks English.
  * ✅ He studies math.
  * ❌ He studies the math.

### 3. **Meals, sports, and transport (general meaning)**

* Don’t use an article when talking **in general**.
* **Example:**

  * ✅ We eat breakfast at 7.
  * ✅ I play football every Sunday.
  * ✅ I go to school by bus.

### 4. **Abstract ideas**

* Don’t use articles with ideas or feelings in general.
* **Example:**

  * ✅ Freedom is important.
  * ✅ Love makes people happy.

---

**Common mistakes:**

* Adding “the” before general things: ❌ I like the music. ✅ I like music.
* Using “a” with uncountable nouns: ❌ I need a water. ✅ I need water.
* Adding “the” before countries or languages: ❌ I visited the Sweden. ✅ I visited Sweden.
