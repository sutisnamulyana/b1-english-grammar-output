## Using No Article (Ø) – B1 Level

**Rule:** Some nouns do **not need an article**.

**When to use no article:**

1. **Plural or uncountable nouns in general:**

   * I like **apples**.
   * She loves **music**.
2. **Names of countries, cities, languages, subjects, meals, and professions:**

   * He studies **English**.
   * We live in **Sweden**.
3. **Days, months, holidays, and seasons (sometimes):**

   * I was born in **March**.
   * We go skiing in **winter**.
4. **After “go”, “at”, “by”, “on” for places/transport (sometimes):**

   * I go to **school**.
   * She travels by **bus**.

**Tip:** If you mean something **specific**, use “the.”
