### Articles with Geographical Names

1. **No article**

* Most countries, cities, towns, lakes, and mountains.
* **Example:** I visited **France**. She lives in **Paris**.

2. **Use "the"**

* Rivers, seas, oceans, mountain ranges, deserts, and groups of islands.
* **Example:** We sailed on **the Nile**. He climbed **the Alps**.

3. **Exceptions**

* Some countries have “the” (plural or descriptive names).
* **Example:** **The Netherlands**, **The United States**.

**Tip:** If the name is singular and just a place, usually no article. If it describes a group or body of water, use **the**.

---

You can practice by looking at maps and noticing which names need **the**.
