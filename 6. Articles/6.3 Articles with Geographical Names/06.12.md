Here’s a clear explanation for a B1 learner about articles with geographical names:

---

### 1. **No article for most countries and cities**

* Use no **the** with names of **countries, cities, towns, and continents**.
* **Example:** I live in **Sweden**. She visited **Paris**.

**Common mistake:** Saying *the France* or *the Tokyo*.

---

### 2. **Use “the” with countries with plural names or words like “kingdom”/“states”**

* Use **the** for countries with plural forms or official titles.
* **Example:** **the Netherlands**, **the United States**, **the United Kingdom**.

---

### 3. **Use “the” for rivers, oceans, seas, and mountain ranges**

* **Example:** **the Nile**, **the Pacific Ocean**, **the Alps**.

**Common mistake:** Saying *Nile River* without **the** when it’s in a sentence: *I swam in the Nile.*

---

### 4. **No article for single mountains, lakes, or islands**

* **Example:** Mount Everest, Lake Victoria, Bali.

**Common mistake:** Saying *the Mount Everest* or *the Bali*.

---

### 5. **Use “the” for deserts, peninsulas, and regions**

* **Example:** **the Sahara**, **the Iberian Peninsula**, **the Middle East**.

---

### ✅ Quick tips

* Think: **plural or official?** → use **the**.
* Think: **single name?** → usually no **the**.
* Remember common exceptions like **the Netherlands** or **the United States**.
