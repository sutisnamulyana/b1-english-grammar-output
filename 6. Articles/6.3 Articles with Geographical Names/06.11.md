Here’s a clear and simple explanation for B1 learners about **articles with geographical names**:

---

## 1. **No Article**

We **don’t use “a” or “the”** with:

* **Most countries**: *France, Japan, Sweden*
* **Cities**: *Paris, Stockholm, Jakarta*
* **Continents**: *Asia, Europe, Africa*
* **Mountains (single)**: *Mount Everest, Mount Fuji*

**Example:**

* I want to visit **Italy** next summer.
* **Tokyo** is very crowded.

---

## 2. **Use “the”**

We **use “the”** with:

* **Countries with plural names**: *the Netherlands, the Philippines*
* **Rivers, seas, oceans**: *the Nile, the Atlantic Ocean*
* **Mountain ranges**: *the Alps, the Rockies*
* **Deserts**: *the Sahara, the Gobi*

**Example:**

* We sailed across **the Mediterranean Sea**.
* He climbed **the Andes** last year.

---

### Tip for learners:

* If it’s a **single country or city**, usually **no article**.
* If it’s a **group of islands, mountains, or rivers**, usually **use “the”**.
