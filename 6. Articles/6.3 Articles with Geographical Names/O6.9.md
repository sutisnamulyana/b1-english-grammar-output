Here’s a simple explanation for a B1 learner about articles with geographical names:

---

## 1. **No Article for Most Places**

**Rule:** We usually **do not use "a" or "the"** with countries, cities, towns, or continents.
**Explanation:** These names are already specific, so an article is not needed.
**Example:**

* I live in **Sweden**.
* She visited **Paris** last summer.
* **Asia** is very big.

---

## 2. **Use "the" with Some Countries**

**Rule:** Use **"the"** with countries that have words like *kingdom*, *states*, *republic*, or are plural.
**Explanation:** These names describe a group of regions or states, so "the" is needed.
**Example:**

* I want to visit **the United States**.
* **The Netherlands** is famous for its windmills.
* He moved to **the United Kingdom** for work.

---

## 3. **Use "the" with Rivers, Seas, Oceans, and Mountain Ranges**

**Rule:** Use **"the"** with these geographical features.
**Explanation:** They are considered unique objects or collections.
**Example:**

* We sailed on **the Nile**.
* They swam in **the Mediterranean Sea**.
* He climbed **the Alps** last year.

---

## 4. **No Article with Lakes and Single Mountains**

**Rule:** We **do not use "the"** with most lakes and individual mountains.
**Example:**

* They went fishing in **Lake Victoria**.
* We hiked up **Mount Everest**.
