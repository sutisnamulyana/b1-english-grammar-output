# O4.3
Here’s a simple explanation of the **passive voice** for a B1 English learner:

---

## **1. What is the Passive Voice?**

The **passive voice** focuses on **the action** or **the person/thing affected** rather than who does it.

* Active: *The chef cooks the meal.*
* Passive: *The meal is cooked by the chef.*

---

## **2. How to Form the Passive Voice**

**Structure:**
**[object] + be + past participle (+ by [subject])**

**Examples by tense:**

| Tense              | Passive Example                            |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Present Simple     | The book **is read** by many students.     |
| Past Simple        | The homework **was done** by the children. |
| Future Simple      | The project **will be finished** tomorrow. |
| Present Continuous | The house **is being cleaned** now.        |
| Past Continuous    | The car **was being repaired** yesterday.  |
| Present Perfect    | The letters **have been sent**.            |

> Note: “by [subject]” is optional if the doer is unknown or unimportant.

---

## **3. When to Use the Passive Voice**

1. **Focus on the action or result:**

   * *The window was broken.* (focus on the broken window, not who did it)
2. **When the doer is unknown:**

   * *The money was stolen.*
3. **Formal writing or reports:**

   * *The experiment was conducted carefully.*

---

## **4. Common Learner Mistakes**

* Forgetting **“be”**: *The cake baked* → ❌ *should be* *The cake **was baked***
* Using wrong **tense**: Match the tense of the active sentence.
* Overusing “by” when unnecessary.
