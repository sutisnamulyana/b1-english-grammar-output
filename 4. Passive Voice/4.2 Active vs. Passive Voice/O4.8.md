# O4.8
Here’s a simple explanation for a B1 learner:

---

## **Active Voice**

**Rule:** The subject does the action.
**Structure:** Subject + Verb + Object
**Example:** *The teacher teaches the students.*

**Common mistakes:**

* Forgetting the subject: *Teaches the students.* ❌
* Confusing object with subject: *The students teach the teacher.* ❌

---

## **Passive Voice**

**Rule:** The action happens to the subject. The doer may be added with “by.”
**Structure:** Subject + be + past participle (+ by + doer)
**Example:** *The students are taught by the teacher.*

**Common mistakes:**

* Forgetting the verb “be”: *The students taught by the teacher.* ❌
* Wrong tense in “be”: *The students is taught by the teacher.* ❌
* Using passive when active is simpler: *The students are taught English every day.* ✅ (good) vs *English is taught to the students every day.* (less natural sometimes)

---

**Tip:**
Use **active** for clear, strong sentences.
Use **passive** when the doer is unknown or not important.
