# O4.7
Here’s a clear explanation for a B1 learner:

---

## **Active Voice**

**Rule:** The subject does the action.
**Structure:** Subject + Verb + Object
**Example:** *The chef cooks the meal.*
**Use:** Shows who is doing the action clearly.

---

## **Passive Voice**

**Rule:** The action is done to the subject. The doer may be optional.
**Structure:** Subject + “be” + Past Participle (+ by + doer)
**Example:** *The meal is cooked by the chef.*
**Use:** Focuses on the action or result, not who did it.

---

**Tip:**

* Active is direct and simple.
* Passive is useful when the doer is unknown or unimportant.
