Here’s a simple explanation for a B1 learner:

---

### Active Voice

**Rule:** The subject does the action.
**Usage:** Use it when you want to focus on who is doing something.
**Example:** *The teacher explains the lesson.*
*(The teacher = subject, explains = verb, the lesson = object)*

### Passive Voice

**Rule:** The object of the action becomes the subject. Use “be + past participle.”
**Usage:** Use it when the action or result is more important than who does it.
**Example:** *The lesson is explained by the teacher.*
*(The lesson = subject, is explained = passive verb, by the teacher = agent)*

**Tip:** Not all sentences need passive voice; overuse can make writing unclear.
