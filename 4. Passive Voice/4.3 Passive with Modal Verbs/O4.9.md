# O4.9
Here’s a clear B1-level explanation of using the **passive with modal verbs**:

---

## **1. Rule**

**Structure:**

```
Subject + modal verb + be + past participle
```

* The **modal verb** can be: can, could, must, should, might, etc.
* **Past participle** is the same as in normal passive sentences.

**Example:**

* Active: Someone **must clean** the room.
* Passive: The room **must be cleaned**.

---

## **2. Usage Explanation**

We use the passive with modals when:

1. **The action is important, but we don’t know or don’t need to mention the doer.**

   * Example: The homework **should be done** before class.

2. **Giving instructions or obligations without naming who must do it.**

   * Example: All documents **must be signed** today.

3. **Talking about possibility or ability without focusing on the person.**

   * Example: The problem **can be solved** easily.

---

## **3. Examples**

| Modal  | Active                            | Passive                           |
| ------ | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| must   | They **must finish** the project. | The project **must be finished**. |
| should | You **should check** your work.   | Your work **should be checked**.  |
| can    | We **can repair** the car.        | The car **can be repaired**.      |

---

**Tip for B1 learners:**

* Always remember: **modal + be + past participle**.
* Don’t forget the **be**! Many learners forget it: ❌ “The room must cleaned” → ✅ “The room must be cleaned”.
