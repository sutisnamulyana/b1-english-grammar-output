# O4.12
Here’s a clear explanation for B1 learners about using **passive with modal verbs**, with a focus on common mistakes:

---

## 1. Rule

When you use a **modal verb** (can, must, should, will, etc.) in passive, the structure is:

**modal + be + past participle**

Example:

* Active: Someone **must clean** the room.
* Passive: The room **must be cleaned**.

---

## 2. Usage

* Use it when you **don’t want to say who does the action**.
* The **focus is on the action**, not the person.

Examples:

* Active: People **should wear** helmets.

* Passive: Helmets **should be worn**.

* Active: They **can fix** the car tomorrow.

* Passive: The car **can be fixed** tomorrow.

---

## 3. Common Mistakes

1. **Leaving out “be”**

* ❌ The car **can fixed** tomorrow.
* ✅ The car **can be fixed** tomorrow.

2. **Using wrong tense of past participle**

* ❌ The homework **must be do**.
* ✅ The homework **must be done**.

3. **Confusing active and passive word order**

* ❌ Must be the room cleaned.
* ✅ The room **must be cleaned**.
