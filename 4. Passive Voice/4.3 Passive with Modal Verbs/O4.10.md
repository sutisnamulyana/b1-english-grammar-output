# O4.10
# Passive with Modal Verbs (B1)

**Rule:**
Modal + be + past participle
Example: *The report **must be finished** by tomorrow.*

**Usage:**
Use passive with modals when the focus is on the action or the object, not who does it.

**Common modals:** must, should, can, may, might, will

**Examples:**

* Active: *They **can complete** the project on time.*

* Passive: *The project **can be completed** on time.*

* Active: *Someone **should check** the email.*

* Passive: *The email **should be checked**.*

**Tip:** Keep the modal unchanged; add **be** + past participle.

This helps emphasize the action, not the doer.
