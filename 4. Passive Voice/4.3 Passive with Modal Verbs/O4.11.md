## **Passive with Modal Verbs**

### **Rule**

Modal verbs (can, should, must, might, etc.) + passive = **modal + be + past participle**.

**Structure:**

```
Subject + modal + be + past participle + (by …)
```

### **When to Use**

* To focus on the action, **not the person doing it**.
* To be more **formal or polite**.

### **Examples**

1. **Active:** They must finish the report.
   **Passive:** The report must be finished (by them).

2. **Active:** Someone should clean the kitchen.
   **Passive:** The kitchen should be cleaned (by someone).

3. **Active:** They can deliver the package tomorrow.
   **Passive:** The package can be delivered tomorrow.

**Tip:** The agent (by someone) is often **optional** if it’s clear or not important.
