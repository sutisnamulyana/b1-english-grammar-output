## **Defining Relative Clauses**

### **Rule**

A defining relative clause gives important information about a noun.
It is **necessary** to understand which person or thing we mean.
We usually use **who**, **which**, or **that**.

* **Who** → people
* **Which** → things or animals
* **That** → people, things, or animals (informal)

**Structure:**
**Noun + relative pronoun + verb**

---

### **Usage Explanation**

* We use defining relative clauses to **identify** or **describe** something.
* **No commas** are used.
* The clause cannot be removed without changing the meaning.

---

### **Examples**

1. **People**:

   * The man **who is wearing a blue jacket** is my uncle.
     (We need to know which man.)

2. **Things**:

   * I like books **that have pictures**.
     (We need to know which books.)

3. **Animals**:

   * The dog **which barked all night** belongs to my neighbor.
