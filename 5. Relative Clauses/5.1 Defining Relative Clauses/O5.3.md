## **Defining Relative Clauses**

### **Rule**

A defining relative clause gives essential information about a person, thing, or place. Without it, the sentence would be unclear.

**Structure:**
**[Main clause] + [relative pronoun] + [extra information]**

**Common relative pronouns:**

* **who** → people
* **which** → things
* **that** → people or things
* **where** → places

### **Usage**

* Use a defining relative clause to describe **exactly which person, thing, or place** you mean.
* No commas are used.

### **Examples**

1. The man **who is wearing a blue shirt** is my teacher.
2. I like the book **that you gave me**.
3. This is the restaurant **where we first met**.
4. She has a dog **which loves to swim**.

**Tip:** If you remove the clause, the meaning changes or becomes unclear.
