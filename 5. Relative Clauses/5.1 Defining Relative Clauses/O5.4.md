## **Defining Relative Clauses**

### **What it is**

A defining relative clause gives important information about a person, thing, or place. Without it, the sentence would be unclear.

### **Words we use**

* **who** → for people
* **which** → for things
* **that** → for people or things

### **Structure**

**Main sentence + relative word + extra information**
Example:

* The man **who is wearing a blue hat** is my uncle.
* I like the book **that you gave me**.

### **Common learner mistakes**

1. **Using commas** – Don’t put a comma in a defining clause.
   ❌ I like the book, **that you gave me**.
   ✅ I like the book **that you gave me**.

2. **Wrong relative word** – Use **who** for people, **which** for things.
   ❌ The girl **which** sings well is my sister.
   ✅ The girl **who** sings well is my sister.

3. **Omitting the relative word incorrectly** – You can omit **that** but not **who**.
   ✅ The book **that** I read was amazing. → The book I read was amazing.
   ❌ The girl I sings well is my sister.
