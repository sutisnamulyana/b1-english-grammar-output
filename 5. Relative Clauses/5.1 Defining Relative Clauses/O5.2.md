### Defining Relative Clauses (B1)

**Rule:** A defining relative clause gives essential information about a noun. Without it, the sentence’s meaning would be unclear.

**Structure:**

* Use **who** for people → *The man **who lives next door** is friendly.*
* Use **which** for things → *I like the book **which you gave me**.*
* Use **that** for people or things → *She wore the dress **that I bought**.*

**Usage:**

* No commas are used.
* It tells **which person or thing** we mean.
* It cannot be removed without changing the meaning.

**Example:**
*The car **that is parked outside** belongs to my brother.*
