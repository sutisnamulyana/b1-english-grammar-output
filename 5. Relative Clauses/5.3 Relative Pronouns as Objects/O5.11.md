## **Omitting Relative Pronouns**

### **Rule**

You can omit the relative pronoun **(who, which, that)** **when it is the object** of the relative clause, but **not when it is the subject**.

### **Usage**

* The pronoun is **optional** if it refers to the object of the clause.
* You **cannot omit** it if it refers to the subject.

### **Examples**

1. **Object (can omit):**

   * With pronoun: *The book **that** I bought is new.*
   * Omitted: *The book I bought is new.* ✅

2. **Subject (cannot omit):**

   * *The man **who** called me is my uncle.* ❌ (cannot say *The man called me is my uncle*)

### **Tips**

* Ask: “Is the pronoun doing the action in the clause?” → If yes, **keep it**.
* If it receives the action → **can omit**.
