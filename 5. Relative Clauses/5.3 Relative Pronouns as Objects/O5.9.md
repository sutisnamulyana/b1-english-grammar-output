## **Omitting Relative Pronouns**

### **Rule**

You can **omit the relative pronoun** (who, which, that) **when it is the object of the relative clause**, but **not when it is the subject**.

### **Usage Explanation**

* **Object:** The relative pronoun receives the action of the verb in the clause.
  → You **can leave it out**.
* **Subject:** The relative pronoun does the action.
  → You **cannot leave it out**.

### **Examples**

**1. Object (can omit)**

* Full: *The book **that I read** was interesting.*
* Omitted: *The book I read was interesting.*

**2. Subject (cannot omit)**

* Correct: *The man **who called** is my teacher.*
* ❌ Wrong: *The man called is my teacher.* (sounds wrong)
