### Non-Defining Relative Clauses (B1)

**Rule:** Use **who, which, whose, where, when** to give extra information about a person, thing, or time.
**Punctuation:** Always use **commas** to separate the clause from the main sentence.

**Usage:** The information is **not essential** to understand the main sentence. You can remove it, and the sentence still makes sense.

**Examples:**

* My brother, **who lives in Sweden**, is a teacher.
* I visited Paris, **which is the capital of France**, last summer.
* We stayed at a hotel, **where I lost my wallet**.

✅ Tip: Don’t use “that” in non-defining clauses.
