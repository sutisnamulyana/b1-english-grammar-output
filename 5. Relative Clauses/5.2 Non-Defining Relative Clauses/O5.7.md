## **Non-Defining Relative Clauses**

### **Rule**

A non-defining relative clause adds extra information about a person, place, or thing.

* It **does not change the main meaning** of the sentence.
* It is **always separated by commas**.
* Common relative pronouns: **who, which, whose, where**.

### **Usage**

* Use it to give **extra details**.
* The sentence would still make sense **without the clause**.

### **Example**

* My brother, **who lives in London**, is a teacher.
  *(Extra info: He lives in London. The main sentence: My brother is a teacher.)*
* Paris, **which is the capital of France**, is very beautiful.
