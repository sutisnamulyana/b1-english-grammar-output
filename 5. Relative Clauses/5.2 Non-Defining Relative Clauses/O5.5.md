Here’s a clear explanation of **non-defining relative clauses** for a B1 learner:

---

## **Rule**

A non-defining relative clause gives extra information about a person, place, or thing.

* It **does not change the main meaning of the sentence**.
* It is **always separated by commas**.
* Common relative pronouns: **who, which, whose, where, when**.
* **You cannot use “that”** in non-defining clauses.

---

## **Usage Explanation**

Use non-defining relative clauses to **add extra details** without changing the main sentence.

* The main sentence still makes sense if the clause is removed.
* This is different from defining relative clauses, which are necessary to understand the sentence.

---

## **Examples**

1. My brother, **who lives in Sweden**, is a software developer.
   *(Extra info: he lives in Sweden. The main point is “My brother is a software developer.”)*

2. London, **which is the capital of England**, is very busy.
   *(Extra info: it is the capital. Main sentence: “London is very busy.”)*

3. I met Sarah, **whose father is a teacher**, at the party.
   *(Extra info: her father is a teacher.)*
