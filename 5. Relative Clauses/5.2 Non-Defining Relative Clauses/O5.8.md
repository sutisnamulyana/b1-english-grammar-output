Here’s a clear explanation of **non-defining relative clauses** for a B1 learner:

---

## **Rule**

A **non-defining relative clause** gives extra information about a person, thing, or place.

* It is **not necessary** to understand the main sentence.
* You **always use commas** around it.
* Common words: **who, which, whose, where**.
* You **cannot use "that"** in non-defining clauses.

---

## **Usage**

* Adds extra details.
* Can be removed without changing the main meaning.
* Often used in writing and speaking for clarity.

---

## **Examples**

1. My brother, **who lives in London**, is a doctor.
   → Extra info: he lives in London.
2. I visited Paris, **which is famous for the Eiffel Tower**.
   → Extra info: famous for the Eiffel Tower.
3. Sarah, **whose car is red**, is my neighbor.
   → Extra info: her car is red.

---

## **Common Mistakes**

1. **No commas:**
   ❌ My brother who lives in London is a doctor.
   ✅ My brother, who lives in London, is a doctor.

2. **Using "that":**
   ❌ I visited Paris, that is famous for the Eiffel Tower.
   ✅ I visited Paris, which is famous for the Eiffel Tower.

3. **Making it necessary info:**
   Remember: non-defining clauses give extra info, not essential info.
