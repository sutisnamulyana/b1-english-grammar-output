Here’s a clear B1-level guide to the **passive voice** in English:

---

## **1. What is the Passive Voice?**

The passive voice focuses on **the action or the object** rather than the person doing it.

* **Active:** The chef cooks the meal.
* **Passive:** The meal **is cooked** by the chef.

---

## **2. How to Form the Passive Voice**

**Structure:**

```
be (in the correct tense) + past participle of the main verb (+ by + doer)
```

* The “doer” (the person doing the action) is optional.

---

## **3. Present Simple Passive**

* **Rule:** am / is / are + past participle
* **Use:** To describe **regular actions or facts**.
* **Example:** The letters **are delivered** every morning.

---

## **4. Past Simple Passive**

* **Rule:** was / were + past participle
* **Use:** To describe actions that **happened in the past**.
* **Example:** The car **was repaired** yesterday.

---

## **5. Present Continuous Passive**

* **Rule:** am / is / are + being + past participle
* **Use:** To describe actions happening **right now**.
* **Example:** The house **is being painted** at the moment.

---

## **6. Past Continuous Passive**

* **Rule:** was / were + being + past participle
* **Use:** To describe actions that **were in progress in the past**.
* **Example:** The documents **were being checked** when I arrived.

---

## **7. Present Perfect Passive**

* **Rule:** has / have + been + past participle
* **Use:** To describe actions **completed recently or with result now**.
* **Example:** The report **has been finished**.

---

## **8. Future Passive**

* **Rule:** will + be + past participle
* **Use:** To talk about actions **that will happen in the future**.
* **Example:** The room **will be cleaned** tomorrow.

---

## **9. Common Mistakes**

1. Forgetting **“be”** → ❌ The meal cooked by the chef. ✅ The meal **is cooked** by the chef.
2. Using the wrong **tense of “be”** → ❌ The letters was delivered. ✅ The letters **were delivered**.
3. Always include the **past participle** of the main verb.
