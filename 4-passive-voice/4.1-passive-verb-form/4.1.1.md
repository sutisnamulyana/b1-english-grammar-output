# Passive Voice (B1 English Level)

For a **B1 English learner**, the **passive voice** is used when the **action is more important than the person who does it**, or when we **don’t know who did the action**.

---

## 1. Basic Idea of Passive Voice

**Active voice**: The subject **does** the action.  
**Passive voice**: The subject **receives** the action.

Example:

Active:  
- The chef cooks the food.

Passive:  
- The food is cooked (by the chef).

In the passive sentence, **“the food” becomes the subject**.

---

## 2. Structure of Passive Voice

The structure is:

**Subject + be (correct tense) + past participle (V3)**

Examples of verbs:

| Base Verb | Past Participle |
|-----------|-----------------|
| make | made |
| write | written |
| build | built |
| clean | cleaned |

Example:

Active:  
- Someone cleaned the room.

Passive:  
- The room **was cleaned**.

---

## 3. Passive in Common Tenses (B1 Level)

### Present Simple
**am / is / are + past participle**

Active:
- They build houses.

Passive:
- Houses **are built**.

Example:
- The company makes cars.  
- Cars **are made** by the company.

---

### Past Simple
**was / were + past participle**

Active:
- Someone stole my bike.

Passive:
- My bike **was stolen**.

Example:
- They built this bridge in 1990.  
- This bridge **was built** in 1990.

---

### Future (will)
**will be + past participle**

Active:
- They will finish the project tomorrow.

Passive:
- The project **will be finished** tomorrow.

---

### Present Perfect
**has / have been + past participle**

Active:
- They have repaired the road.

Passive:
- The road **has been repaired**.

---

## 4. Using “by” (optional)

We use **“by + person”** if we want to say **who did the action**.

Examples:

- The book **was written by J.K. Rowling**.  
- The painting **was made by a famous artist**.

Often we **do not mention the person**:

- The window **was broken**.  
- The email **was sent yesterday**.

---

## 5. When Passive Voice Is Common

Passive is often used for:

### 1. News
- A bank **was robbed** yesterday.

### 2. Processes
- Coffee **is grown** in Brazil.

### 3. Formal writing
- The experiment **was conducted** in 2022.

---

## 6. Quick Formula to Convert Active → Passive

Steps:

1. Move the **object** to the beginning.
2. Add the correct form of **be**.
3. Use the **past participle (V3)**.

Example:

Active:  
- The teacher **explains** the lesson.

Steps:
- Object → *the lesson*  
- Add **is** + explained

Passive:

- The lesson **is explained** (by the teacher).

---

## Simple Summary

Passive voice structure:

**Subject + be + past participle**

Examples:

- The cake **is made** every day.  
- The car **was repaired** yesterday.  
- The meeting **will be held** tomorrow.
