## 1. What Is the Passive Voice?

The **passive voice** is used when the **focus is on the action**, not on the person who does the action.

- **Active voice:** The subject **does** the action.  
- **Passive voice:** The subject **receives** the action.

**Example**

Active:  
- The chef cooks the meal.

Passive:  
- The meal **is cooked** by the chef.

Here, the focus is **the meal**, not the chef.

---

## 2. How to Form the Passive Voice

### Rule

**Subject + form of “be” + past participle (V3)**

| Tense | Form of "be" | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present | am / is / are | The book **is written** by her |
| Past | was / were | The book **was written** by her |

**Past participle examples**

| Verb | Past participle |
|---|---|
| write | written |
| make | made |
| build | built |
| cook | cooked |

---

## 3. Passive Voice in the Present Simple

### Rule
**Subject + am / is / are + past participle**

### Usage
Used for **general facts, routines, or regular actions**.

### Example

Active:  
- People **speak** English all over the world.

Passive:  
- English **is spoken** all over the world.

Another example:  
- The reports **are prepared** every week.

---

## 4. Passive Voice in the Past Simple

### Rule
**Subject + was / were + past participle**

### Usage
Used for **actions that happened in the past** when the doer is not important or unknown.

### Example

Active:  
- Someone **stole** my bike.

Passive:  
- My bike **was stolen** yesterday.

Another example:  
- The bridge **was built** in 1998.

---

## 5. Passive Voice in the Present Continuous

### Rule
**Subject + am / is / are + being + past participle**

### Usage
Used for **actions happening right now**.

### Example

Active:  
- The workers **are repairing** the road.

Passive:  
- The road **is being repaired**.

---

## 6. Passive Voice in the Present Perfect

### Rule
**Subject + has / have + been + past participle**

### Usage
Used for actions that **happened in the past but are important now**.

### Example

Active:  
- The company **has released** a new product.

Passive:  
- A new product **has been released**.

---

## 7. When to Use the Passive Voice

Passive voice is common when:

### 1. The doer is unknown
Example:  
- My wallet **was stolen**.

### 2. The action is more important than the person
Example:  
- The law **was passed** in 2020.

### 3. In formal or scientific writing
Example:  
- The experiment **was conducted** in three stages.

---

## 8. Using “by” to Show the Doer

If you want to mention who did the action, use **by + person**.

Example:

Active:  
- Shakespeare wrote *Hamlet*.

Passive:  
- *Hamlet* **was written by Shakespeare**.

Often the **“by” phrase is not necessary**.

Example:  
- The window **was broken**. (We don’t know who broke it.)

---

## 9. Quick Comparison

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| The teacher explains the lesson. | The lesson **is explained** by the teacher. |
| Someone cleaned the room. | The room **was cleaned**. |
| They are building a bridge. | A bridge **is being built**. |

---

✅ **Simple Tip for B1 learners**

If you see:  
**be + past participle (V3)**  

Example:  
- is made  
- was built  
- has been finished  

It is **probably passive voice**.
