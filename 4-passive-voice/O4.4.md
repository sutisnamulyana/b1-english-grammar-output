Here’s a clear guide for B1 learners on **passive voice** with simple language and focus on common mistakes:

---

## 1. What is passive voice?

In passive voice, the **object of the action becomes the subject of the sentence**.
Example:

* Active: *The chef cooks the meal.*
* Passive: *The meal is cooked by the chef.*

---

## 2. How to form it

**Structure:**
**Be + past participle (+ by …)**

| Tense              | Example                                    | Common mistake                                            |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Present simple     | The book **is read** by many students.     | Forgetting "is/are" → *The book read by many students.* ❌ |
| Past simple        | The song **was sung** by her.              | Using base verb → *The song was sing by her.* ❌           |
| Present continuous | The house **is being painted** by workers. | Omitting "being" → *The house is painted by workers.* ❌   |
| Future             | The work **will be done** tomorrow.        | Using wrong form → *The work will done tomorrow.* ❌       |

---

## 3. When to use passive

1. **When we don’t know who does the action:**

* *My wallet was stolen.* (We don’t know the thief.)

2. **When the action is more important than who does it:**

* *The bridge was built in 1990.*

---

## 4. Common mistakes

1. **Wrong verb form:**

* ❌ *The cake is eat by the children.*
* ✅ *The cake is eaten by the children.*

2. **Forgetting “be” form:**

* ❌ *The letter written by Anna.*
* ✅ *The letter **was** written by Anna.*

3. **Overusing “by” when it’s not needed:**

* ❌ *The homework is done by me every day.* (correct, but “by me” is often unnecessary)
* ✅ *The homework is done every day.*

4. **Mixing tenses incorrectly:**

* ❌ *The movie is watched yesterday.*
* ✅ *The movie was watched yesterday.*
